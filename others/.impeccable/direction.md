# Tailor shop workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: login and all inventory workspace routes. Primary target: admin/frontend/src/App.tsx.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A tailor can find a material and record its use without translating between a spreadsheet and the shelf. Preserve the established administrative vocabulary and keep job references beside stock changes.

OWN-WORLD: Brown navigation rail, amber selected state and chart series, warm light canvas, white table surfaces, Geist workhorse type, fine warm borders. A lit shop desk under daylight calls for a light work area with strong ink contrast. Use state color only for meaningful stock statuses.

STORY: The owner sees what needs replenishment, finds the exact fabric/color/unit, and receives or issues the correct amount. Spreadsheet work starts with a visible preview and ends with an explicit confirmation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed 232px brown sidebar at desktop; slim workspace/date/account header; page title and action on the same row; four aligned stock facts; a wide material-movement chart beside a low-stock list; recent transactions below. Inventory leads with search, category/status filters, a compact material register and an anchored record drawer. Signature interaction: the drawer previews the actual remaining balance as a fabric quantity is entered, with meters always visible.

FORM: Existing tailor shop administrative design. Preserve its established palette and navigation. Code-first because this is an app screen. No approved image comp; seed:2323ac0efcb13c62.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, docs/DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Open: original Excel sheet/column mapping. The current template is provisional.

## Provenance

The established brown/amber design remains the visual authority; external design reference materials are no longer retained, and catalog challengers were not adopted.
